ABOUT US

Who We Are

King's Dominion Outreach Ministry (KDOM) is a fire brand church committed to winning souls, nurturing believers, and impacting communities with the love and truth of Jesus Christ.

We believe in the transforming power of God to restore lives, strengthen families, and raise disciples who will advance God's Kingdom through service, outreach, and leadership.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To reach all 36 states of Nigeria including Abuja, all the African nations and the six continents of the world with the message of Christ through evangelism, crusade, outreaches and church planting. Acts 1 vs 8

Our Vision

Carrying the ark of the lord of all the earth throughout the whole earth Joshua 3 vs 13

OUR OBJECTIVES

Evangelism of the world through crusade and outreaches

Raising and building excellent lives through message of hope and mystery of life making.

Raising the Gideon army of this end time through bible training and leadership development programs.

Showing the love of Christ to our generation through empowerment programs and vocational training.

Bringing the life and hope of Christ to the people of our generation through publications.

Raising teenagers and youths of destiny
